Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or other sensitive financial credentials — you enter your spending data manually.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expense history, and generating summaries and insights about your spending patterns. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font delivery services) to operate the Service. These providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, as part of delivering their services. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We're happy to help clarify how your information is used or to process a data deletion request.
